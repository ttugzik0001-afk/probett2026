Our Story

It began with a shared table and a very long evening.

We met in a small bookshop in Aix-en-Provence — Bethel was reaching for the last copy of a poetry collection, and Promise had already tucked it under her arm. We talked until the shopkeeper politely turned the lights off.

Six years, three cities and countless cups of coffee later, we are returning to the place where it all started. We would be honoured to have you beside us as we say yes to a lifetime of long evenings together.
